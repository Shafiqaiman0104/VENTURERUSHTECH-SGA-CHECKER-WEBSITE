Definitions & Standards

Key SEO, AEO & GEO Metrics Explained

Understanding these three core definitions helps marketing and engineering teams prioritize fixes that actually lift rankings and AI citations:

SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Technical and on-page engineering that helps Google and Bing crawl, index, and rank your pages for high-intent searches.
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Structuring direct 20–60 word answers, question subheadings, tables, and schema so answer engines quote your site directly in AI Overviews.
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Strengthening factual statistics, citations, entity markup, and /llms.txt files so large language models recommend your brand accurately.
